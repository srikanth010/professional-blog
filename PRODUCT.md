# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors to Srikanth's professional portfolio are primarily:
- Engineering leaders and talent scouts evaluating expertise and hiring potential
- Peers in the AEM/content platform space seeking technical insights
- Potential clients or collaborators interested in consulting or partnership
- Students or junior engineers learning from technical writing and experience

They visit to assess credibility, explore technical depth, and potentially initiate professional relationships. Context ranges from quick portfolio review to deep blog exploration during research sprints.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio is Srikanth's professional identity online. It serves to:
- Demonstrate 7+ years of depth in AEM, headless CMS, and enterprise content platforms
- Share technical insights through a public blog
- Provide an approachable way for potential collaborators and employers to learn about expertise
- Position as a forward-thinking technologist pushing the boundaries of what modern CMS can do

Success means: visitors leave with clear confidence in technical capability, understand the specific value offered, and have a direct path to initiate contact.

## Brand Personality

**Bold innovator** who is measured and thoughtful. Not reckless; ambitious and forward-looking. Pushes boundaries in enterprise tech, solves hard problems, and isn't constrained by "the way things have always been done." Approachable and willing to share knowledge. The voice is expert-confident without gatekeeping.

**Three words:** Ambitious, Thoughtful, Pioneering

**Emotional goals:**
- Inspire confidence in technical depth
- Signal forward-thinking and innovation without appearing untested
- Feel like learning from a peer, not reading marketing

## Anti-references

- **Corporate/stiff formality.** Avoid: business-card layouts, conservative color palettes, sterile "about me" copy
- **Trendy flashiness.** Avoid: animations for animation's sake, constant motion that distracts, effects that fade in 6 months
- **Generic SaaS defaults.** Avoid: cream/warm beige backgrounds, purple accents with predictable gradients, identical card grids, tiny all-caps eyebrows above every section

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over spectacle.** Motion and effects amplify message; they never obscure it. Every animation earns its place.
2. **Expertise signals quality.** Craft visible in spacing, typography, and interaction. Details compound into impression of thoughtfulness.
3. **Dark theme is intentional.** Not "cool dark" by default, but chosen for the context: technical portfolio, code-forward audience, late-night reading comfort.
4. **Content-first layout.** Blog and writing are primary; portfolio frames the writing. Navigation and structure should never compete with the words.
5. **Restrained, branded color.** Purple accent is committed and intentional, not a reflex. Palette is disciplined, not maximalist.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG AA compliance** (minimum). Contrast ratios ≥4.5:1 for body text, ≥3:1 for large text.
- **Reduced motion respected.** Animations have non-motion fallbacks; critical content is not gated on transitions.
- **Semantic HTML and keyboard navigation** throughout.
- **Dark theme maintained with proper contrast** even for muted/secondary text.
